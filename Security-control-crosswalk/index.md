---
title: Security control crosswalk
---
This project is a control crosswalk that maps 10 common security controls (such as access control, MFA, encryption, incident response, and vendor management) to SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, PCI DSS v4.0.1, and GDPR, showing how one control can satisfy requirements in several frameworks at once so a company like the fictional SaaS business Northbridge Analytics can build each control once and reuse the evidence across audits. Each mapping is rated Direct or Supporting and paired with a plain-English explanation and an example of audit evidence, and the analysis finds that encryption, incident response, and vendor management are Direct matches in all four frameworks, while GDPR is outcome-based and names only four of the ten controls directly. It also notes what a crosswalk can’t show, such as ISO 27001’s management system requirements and GDPR’s broader privacy obligations.
