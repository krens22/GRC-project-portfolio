---
title: Supply Chain Risk Assessment - Harborline Health Systems Inc
---

This project is a third-party and supply chain cyber risk assessment for Harborline Health Systems, a fictional Toronto-based health-tech company whose cloud platform handles appointment booking, reminders, and virtual care for about 400 Ontario clinics and 1.5 million patient records. Of the 12 suppliers in Harborline’s inventory, 11 that support the patient platform were tiered by criticality and assessed using a 5×5 likelihood and impact model, measured against ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST SP 800-161, and the NIST CSF 2.0. The assessment identified 27 supplier risks, with none rated Critical and 11 rated High after existing controls, concentrated in an offshore development contractor, a managed services provider with privileged access, and the open-source components inside the platform. The findings trace back to a handful of Harborline-level root causes, including the lack of a standard supplier contract baseline, no review of suppliers after onboarding, and informal access governance, and they lead to a prioritized treatment plan with owners and timelines. Harborline and all suppliers are fictional, and the project was built as a portfolio piece to demonstrate GRC methodology, risk scoring, and professional report writing.
