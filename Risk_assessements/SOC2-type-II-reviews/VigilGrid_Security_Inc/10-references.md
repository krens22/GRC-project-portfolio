## 10. References

### 10.1 Standards and frameworks
- AICPA. *2017 Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy (With Revised Points of Focus, 2022).* Basis for the control references used in this review (CC6.2, CC6.3, CC7.3, CC8.1, CC9.2, A1.2, C1.2).
- AICPA. *SOC 2 Guide: Reporting on an Examination of Controls at a Service Organization Relevant to Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, or Privacy.* Guidance on Type II reports, carve-out and inclusive methods, subservice organizations, and complementary user entity controls.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A, controls 5.19 to 5.22 (supplier relationships and managing supplier services). Context for third-party risk practices.

### 10.2 Privacy and data protection
- European Union. Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation): Article 28 (processors and data processing agreements), Article 32 (security of processing), Article 35 (data protection impact assessment), Chapter V, Articles 44 to 46 (international transfers).
- European Commission. Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 on standard contractual clauses for transfers of personal data to third countries.
- European Commission. Adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (July 10, 2023).
- Ontario. *Employment Standards Act, 2000*, Part XI.1 (written policy on electronic monitoring for employers with 25 or more employees).
- New York State. Civil Rights Law, Section 52-c (notice of electronic monitoring of employees).
- Quebec. *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (as amended by Law 25).
