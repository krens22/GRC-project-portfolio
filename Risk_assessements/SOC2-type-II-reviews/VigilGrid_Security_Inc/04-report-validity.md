## 4. Report validity

| Check | Finding | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Report type** | Type II (tests controls over a period, not just their design at a single date) | Acceptable |
| **Auditor opinion** | **Qualified.** The auditor's opinion is "except for" the failure of access removal controls (CC6.3) | **Concern** |
| **Report currency** | Period ended March 31, 2026; the review date is October 5, 2026, leaving about 6 months with no audit coverage | **Gap** |
| **Period length** | 6 months (many customers expect 12) | **Concern** |
| **Criteria covered** | Security, Availability, Confidentiality. Privacy and Processing Integrity are not covered | **Concern** |

**Validity conclusion:** The report is usable as evidence, but only with conditions. It cannot be relied on alone for a Tier 1 vendor.

**Actions required:**
1. Obtain a bridge letter covering April 1, 2026 to the present.
2. Ask VigilGrid whether this is its first SOC 2 report and whether a 12-month period is planned.
3. Cover privacy through a DPA and a separate privacy review, since the audit doesn't address it.
