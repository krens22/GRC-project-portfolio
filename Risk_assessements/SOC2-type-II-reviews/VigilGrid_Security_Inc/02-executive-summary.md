## 2. Executive summary

Meridian Logistics' security team has asked to engage VigilGrid Security Inc. for 24/7 managed detection and response. Because VigilGrid's software would run with elevated privileges on every Meridian endpoint, the vendor is rated Tier 1 (Critical). This review assessed VigilGrid's SOC 2 Type II report (October 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026) for reliability, scope, exceptions, and customer responsibilities.

**Key findings**
- **The report carries a qualified opinion.** The auditor found that VigilGrid did not remove departed staff's access within its own 24-hour standard, and privileged access reviews were not performed in either quarter of the period. For a vendor with reach into customer environments, this is the most significant weakness.
- **The audit leaves out important pieces.** The Automated Response feature, the mobile app, and privacy practices are not covered, and the overnight monitoring partner, responsible for one third of each day's coverage, was neither audited nor assessed by VigilGrid.
- **The evidence is dated and incomplete.** About six months have passed since the audit period ended, and VigilGrid has not provided a bridge letter, penetration test summary, or data processing agreement.
- **Meridian carries obligations too.** The vendor expects Meridian to maintain agent coverage and its contact list and to ensure monitoring complies with employee privacy laws in Ireland, Canada, and the United States, which is the most demanding obligation.

**Why approve with conditions rather than reject.** The remaining controls tested well, and each gap can be addressed with a practical safeguard, leaving no risk above Medium once measures are in place. Approving without conditions is not supportable for a Tier 1 vendor.

**Conditions before signing:** a bridge letter; evidence that the access removal and review problems are fixed; the overnight partner's risk assessment and assurance report; a signed data processing agreement with a transfer mechanism for EU data; and a penetration test summary. **Before go-live:** a privacy assessment and employee notices, Automated Response in alert-only mode, and no use of the mobile app until audited.

**This recommendation changes to reject** if VigilGrid cannot provide the bridge letter or remediation evidence, if the bridge letter discloses new failures, or if the next report repeats the access exceptions.
