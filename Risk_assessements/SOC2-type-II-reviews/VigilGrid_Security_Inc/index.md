title: SOC Type-II reviews - VigilGrid Security
---

Meridian Logistics, a fictional logistics company with about 1,200 employees across Canada, the US, and Ireland, wants to hire VigilGrid Security Inc., a fictional managed detection and response vendor, to monitor its devices around the clock. Because VigilGrid’s software runs with elevated privileges on every Meridian endpoint, I rated it Tier 1 (Critical) and reviewed its SOC 2 Type II report as a third-party risk analyst would before signing: I checked the report’s validity, mapped what the audit covers and leaves out, rated four control exceptions and judged how credible management’s responses were, analyzed Meridian’s own responsibilities under the report, and built a register of ten gaps with compensating controls, contract terms, and one deliberate risk acceptance. The report carried a qualified opinion over failures to remove departed staff’s access and to review privileged access, and it left the Automated Response feature, the mobile app, privacy practices, and the overnight monitoring partner outside its scope, with no bridge letter or data processing agreement provided. I recommended approving with conditions, with five items required before signing and the rest before go-live, no residual risk above Medium, and clear triggers that would turn the recommendation into a rejection.
