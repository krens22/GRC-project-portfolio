## 3. Method

1. **Collected the vendor's evidence.** Requested PayStream's AOC and Report on Compliance (ROC) through the vendor intake process.
2. **Validated the AOC.** Checked that the document is current (dated within the last 12 months), assessed against the current PCI DSS version (v4.0.1), signed off by a Qualified Security Assessor (QSA), and shows an overall status of "Compliant."
3. **Identified the assessed services.** Read the scope sections of the AOC to see which services PayStream had assessed and which it listed as provided but not assessed.
4. **Compared scope to Larkspur's needs.** Matched each service Larkspur needs (Section 3) against the AOC's scope and marked each as covered, partially covered, or not covered.
5. **Rated the risk of each gap.** Scored each gap by likelihood and impact using a High / Medium / Low scale.
6. **Identified responsibilities.** Noted which PCI DSS requirements PayStream handles and which stay with Larkspur.
7. **Developed recommendations.** Proposed actions to close or manage each gap, and made a decision (approve, approve with conditions, or reject).
