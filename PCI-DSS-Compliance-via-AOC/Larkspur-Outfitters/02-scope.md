## 3. Scope of Review

### Services Larkspur Needs from PayStream

| # | Service | Why Larkspur needs it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-time card processing | Standard online checkout for individual orders |
| 2 | Card-on-file storage | Saving a customer's card so they don't re-enter it each month |
| 3 | Recurring billing | Automatically charging subscribers every month |

### Documents Reviewed

- PayStream Solutions PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance (AOC) for Service Providers, signed March 14, 2026 (fictional)

### Documents Requested but Not Yet Received

- PayStream's Report on Compliance (ROC), the detailed audit behind the AOC

### Basis for the Review

This review supports PCI DSS v4.0.1 Requirement 12.8, which requires organizations to perform due diligence on third-party service providers before engaging them and to track their PCI DSS compliance status.

### Out of Scope

- Technical testing of PayStream's systems (this is a document review only)
- Review of PayStream's financial stability, pricing, or contract terms
- Larkspur's own PCI DSS compliance (covered separately)
